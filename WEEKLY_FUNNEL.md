# Weekly funnel: independent service businesses

## Niche and offer

Focus on solo and small independent service businesses that need a simple way to plan client outreach, customer follow-ups, and weekly delivery. The featured offer is the **Service Business Weekly Planner** ($9), delivered through the existing Stripe checkout and download flow.

The funnel is social content → storefront product card → Stripe checkout → instant download and receipt. The Cash App link is an optional, separate way to support the app; it is not a product payment method or a substitute for checkout.

## Seven-day campaign

| Day | Funnel role | Post prompt and call to action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Awareness | “What is one follow-up that would make this week easier?” Invite service owners to name a customer or lead they need to reconnect with. |
| Tuesday | Helpful tip | Share a three-line weekly plan: outreach, client delivery, and follow-ups. Link to the planner in the storefront. |
| Wednesday | Product demo | Show the planner sections for outreach and customer follow-ups. CTA: “See the Service Business Weekly Planner.” |
| Thursday | Objection handling | Explain that the download is a simple printable worksheet, not another project-management system. Link to the product details. |
| Friday | Conversion | Remind followers the planner is available for $9, with secure checkout and an instant download. |
| Saturday | Engagement | Ask followers which task most often slips: outreach, follow-up, or delivery. Use replies to shape the next week's content; do not imply endorsements. |
| Sunday | Reset | Share a weekly reset prompt and invite people to plan Monday's first outreach task with the planner. |

## Support and consent

Use the site's Cash App support link for voluntary app support only. Cash App handles the payment; the storefront does not receive payment confirmation or issue receipts for those contributions. Keep marketing sign-ups separate from Stripe receipt emails and only send promotional email to people who explicitly opt in.
