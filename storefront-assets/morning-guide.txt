MINDFUL MORNING GUIDE

A gentle start is more useful than a perfect routine.

1. PAUSE
Before reaching for your phone, take three slow breaths. Notice how you feel without judging it.

2. CHOOSE ONE ANCHOR
Pick one small action that helps you feel grounded: drink a glass of water, stretch for two minutes, open a window, or write down a thought.

3. NAME WHAT MATTERS
Write the one thing that would make today feel meaningful. Keep it small enough to begin.

4. MAKE ROOM
Leave a little space between your first commitment and the rest of the day. A routine should support your life, not make it busier.

Try this for a week. Keep what feels helpful and let the rest go.
